How missing journeys are classified

A journey that does not appear in AVL tracking is not automatically lost service. It is checked against vehicle and driver-duty records.

If it ran, it is classed as Operated – AVL non-tracking with cause AVL / GPS data issue. It counts against the tracking rate but carries zero lost km.

If it didn't run, or ran only part of the route, it is Not operated, Partially operated or Stops bypassed. The missing distance is recorded as lost km with its real cause.

This keeps data-quality problems from being reported as service failures, and the other way round.
